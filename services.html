Weddings, prom nights, and black-tie affairs are fitted in-house, with a full range of tuxedo and suit styles, colors, and accessories on hand for every party in the wedding — available to rent or buy.

Please Note

Limited Alteration Availability

All alterations are handled on a limited/minimum-availability basis.

During Prom and Wedding Season (May through June), we are sometimes unable to accommodate everyone, and alteration availability is especially limited. Booking your appointment early is highly recommended.

When calling for bridal alterations, please call at least 3–4 months prior to your event.

For custom items, you're encouraged to inquire well in advance — custom work takes time.

Embroidery
Dept. III — Custom Embroidery

Custom machine embroidery is worked upon elegant fabrics, drawing inspiration from antique textiles and vintage embroidery designs of a bygone era.

Every piece is finished with care and made right here in America, whether a monogram on a handkerchief or a full embellished panel for a wedding gown.

Bring your own garment or fabric, or commission a piece entirely from scratch — either way, the design begins with a conversation about what you have in mind.
